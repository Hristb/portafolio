I'm a **Senior Backend Engineer** with over 6 years of software development experience, specialized in **Java 17**, **Quarkus**, **Spring Boot** and microservices architectures for the **FinTech and Neobanking** sector.

I currently work at **Indra** as part of the **MiBanco Digital Models** team, leading the development of APIs and microservices for a **Neobank (WhatsApp Bank)**. My main role covers designing and building services that connect the digital channel to the **banking core**, following **BIAN** standard guidelines. This includes wallet interoperability (Yape/Mibanco), interbank transfers, card-to-card payments and automation flows with **Azure Data Factory**.

I have participated in multiple projects within the **MiBancoLab** ecosystem, working as a **Fullstack** developer on initiatives such as **Academia de Progreso** (educational platform for microentrepreneurs with Angular and course/certificate management microservices), **Simulador ADN** (critical platform with token-based authentication), **Programa de Puntos y Referidos** (end-to-end loyalty systems) and **YEVO** (loan platform with **ChatBot Botmaker** integration). I also implemented **Azure Personalizer** for course personalization and **Azure Functions** for financial modules and automation.

All development is **AI-driven** — I use **GitHub Copilot** with advanced **Prompt Engineering** practices and the **SDD (Spec-Driven Development)** methodology as a work structure. Additionally, I support the operations team with incident analysis and monitoring using the **ELK Stack** (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana).

Previous experience at **Canvia** (Risk Profiling System for INER), **GLOBAL HITSS** (SIAC System for Claro Perú) and **REVSA - Grupo Credivargas** (KPI Dashboard and internal management systems).

I'm a self-taught, analytical professional with a constant drive for improvement. I'm motivated by building robust, scalable and secure solutions that create real impact in the financial industry. 🚀
