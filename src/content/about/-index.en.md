I've been building software for over 6 years, specialized in **Java 17**, **Quarkus**, **Spring Boot** and microservices architectures for the **FinTech and Neobanking** sector.

I'm currently part of the **MiBanco Digital Models** team at **Indra**, which builds a **Neobank (WhatsApp Bank)**. My contribution is on the **backend**: designing and building services that connect the digital channel to the **banking core**, following **BIAN** standard guidelines — wallet interoperability (Yape/Mibanco), interbank transfers, card-to-card payments and automation flows with **Azure Data Factory**.

I have participated in multiple projects within the **MiBancoLab** ecosystem, working as a **Fullstack** developer on initiatives such as **Academia de Progreso** (educational platform for microentrepreneurs with Angular and course/certificate management microservices), **Simulador ADN** (a tool for MiBanco advisors to track progress toward their sales goals and project, from that progress, whether they'll meet them and what their monthly compensation will look like), **Programa de Puntos y Referidos** (end-to-end loyalty systems) and **YEVO** (loan platform with **ChatBot Botmaker** integration). I also implemented **Azure Personalizer** for course personalization and **Azure Functions** for financial modules and automation.

AI is part of my daily workflow, with whatever tools each company provides — today that includes **GitHub Copilot**, **Claude** and **ChatGPT**, applying **Prompt Engineering** and the **SDD (Spec-Driven Development)** methodology to structure development. It's still engineering work: AI speeds up how I write and document code, it doesn't replace design or technical decisions. I also support the operations team with incident analysis and monitoring in production using **Dynatrace** (I previously worked with the **ELK Stack** too).

Previous experience at **Canvia** (Risk Profiling System for INER), **GLOBAL HITSS** (SIAC System for Claro Perú) and **REVSA - Grupo Credivargas** (KPI Dashboard and internal management systems).

I'm self-taught, analytical, and driven to keep improving. I'm motivated by building robust, scalable and secure solutions that create real impact in the financial industry.
